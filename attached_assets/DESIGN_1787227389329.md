## Brand & Style

The design system is built for a B2B marketplace that prioritizes human connection over corporate distance. The brand personality is **warm, direct, and accessible**, bridging the gap between high-volume manufacturing and boutique e-commerce aesthetics. 

The visual style is a blend of **Minimalism and Tactile Modernism**. It avoids the sterile "SaaS blue" in favor of an organic, earth-toned palette that reflects the physical nature of custom packaging. The interface utilizes generous whitespace, soft rounded corners, and subtle depth to make complex sourcing workflows feel as intuitive as consumer shopping. The emotional response should be one of capability and creative partnership—making the user feel that high-end manufacturing is finally within reach.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by **Surface Cream (#FAF5EA)** to provide a warmer, more premium feel than pure white. This base reduces eye strain and establishes a "paper-like" canvas appropriate for a packaging platform. 

**Terracotta (#C1502E)** serves as the primary action color. It is used exclusively for high-priority CTAs, focus states, and meaningful highlights. This high-contrast pairing against the cream background ensures accessibility while maintaining a sophisticated, artisanal character.

**Sand Secondary (#F0E1CB)** is used for low-contrast containers, such as tags, category chips, and secondary buttons, creating a layered effect without introducing distracting new hues. **Ink Text (#1F1B16)** provides grounded legibility, used for all primary communication and iconography.

## Typography

This design system uses a dual-font strategy. **Plus Jakarta Sans** is the display typeface, chosen for its friendly yet professional geometry. It should be used for all headlines and large titles to inject personality into the platform.

**Inter** is the workhorse for body copy, data tables, and labels. Its high legibility and neutral character balance the more expressive headlines, ensuring that technical specifications and sourcing details remain clear and easy to scan.

For mobile-first optimization, headlines scale down significantly to prevent awkward line breaks. Tighten letter spacing on larger headlines to maintain a cohesive, "editorial" look.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **fluid-to-fixed** hybrid model. On mobile devices, the system uses a 4-column grid with 16px side margins. On desktop, it transitions to a 12-column grid with a maximum content width of 1280px to maintain readability.

Spacing is governed by an 8px linear scale. We prioritize vertical "rhythm" to create a sense of order. In product listings and supplier cards, use generous internal padding (24px) to ensure the interface feels spacious and premium. Elements should be "stacked" with consistent gaps: 12px for related items (label + input), 24px for distinct sections, and 48px for major content blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Hierarchy is established through **Tonal Layers** rather than heavy shadows. The base layer is the Surface Cream background. Cards and interactive containers use a pure white (#FFFFFF) background to "lift" them off the canvas.

Shadows are used sparingly to indicate interactivity. Use a single "Soft Lift" shadow style for cards: `0px 4px 12px rgba(31, 27, 22, 0.05)`. This subtle, slightly tinted shadow prevents the design from feeling flat while maintaining the "accessible e-commerce" aesthetic. On hover, this shadow may slightly intensify to provide tactile feedback.

## Shapes

The shape language is consistently **Rounded**, mirroring the physical curves of packaging and containers. 

- **Standard Elements (Inputs, Small Buttons):** 8px (0.5rem) radius.
- **Large Elements (Cards, Featured Modules):** 16px (1rem) radius.
- **Accent Elements (Tags, Search Bars):** 24px (1.5rem) or fully pill-shaped.

Avoid sharp corners entirely to maintain the approachable, warm brand personality.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary buttons use the Terracotta (#C1502E) background with white text and a 16px (1rem) minimum corner radius for a "friendly" oversized feel. Secondary buttons use the Sand (#F0E1CB) background with Ink text. All buttons should have a minimum height of 48px to be mobile-friendly.

### Cards
Supplier and product cards must include a subtle 1px border using a 10% opacity version of the Ink color. They utilize the 16px corner radius and "Soft Lift" shadow. Card headers should prominently feature the "Star Rating" in Terracotta to emphasize trust.

### Input Fields
Inputs use a white background with a 1px border in Sand. On focus, the border transitions to Terracotta. Labels are always positioned above the input in `label-md` Inter.

### Chips & Tags
Used for material types (e.g., "Corrugated," "Eco-friendly"). These should use the Sand (#F0E1CB) color with `label-sm` typography and a pill-shaped radius.

### Star Ratings
Star icons should be solid-filled in Terracotta when active and Sand when inactive. They are always paired with a numerical value in parentheses for clarity.
